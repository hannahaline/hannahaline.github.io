<p class="hat-lead">
Each line is where the shoreline sat, on average, in one year: 1985, 1995, 2005, 2015 and 2025.
Zoom in on a village to see how far the beach has moved, and switch the photo underneath to see the island as it looked.
</p>

Single satellite images can be off by 10 m or more, and the shoreline moves back and forth with every storm and season.
Averaging all the images from a whole year gives a steadier line to compare from decade to decade.
