<p class="hat-lead">
Hatteras Island today is partly a product of nature and partly a product of people.
For nearly a century, people have built dunes, paved and moved a highway, pumped sand onto beaches, and built structures to hold the shore in place.
Each of these changes how sand moves, and therefore how the island changes.
</p>
