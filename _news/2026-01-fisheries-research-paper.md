Our paper on how dolphins and sea turtles interact with Alabama's commercial shrimping industry is out in <i>Fisheries Research</i>. Interviews with shrimpers show dolphins increasingly raiding nets and scavenging bycatch, while turtle excluder devices have made conflicts with sea turtles rare. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fishres.2025.107645">Read the paper</a>.
